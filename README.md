This is a small web toy I made for my own tall maiden fetish, and; it does some little calculations along the way. You upload a picture of a character, or person, and‑then you tell it how tall she really is in centimetres, then; she takes her place in a line sorted by height against a live ruler down the side, every figure drawn to the same scale so the sizes stay truthfully comparable. Click any of them, and; a panel opens with about twenty body numbers worked out for that height, mass, blood, brain, heart rate, lifespan, voice pitch, & c., all driven by the one ratio k = H / 160 where 160 cm is the World‑average adult woman. That is all it really is, a layout, a ruler, & a pile of physics done as faithfully as I could manage for a thing that is fictional at its root. Wishing a happy day to anyone who wanders past.
